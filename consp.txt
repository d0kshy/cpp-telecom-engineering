•	RAN (Radio Access Network): 
        The part of a telecommunications system that connects individual devices to the core network through radio connections.
•	gNodeB (gNB) / eNodeB (eNB): 
        The 5G and 4G base stations (antennas) that your UE Simulator will test under heavy load.
•	Core Network (5GC / EPC): 
        The central part of the mobile network that handles authentication, routing,and billing.
